# Product Principles

1. **Code-first, UI-operated**
2. **Framework-neutral**
3. **Deterministic first**
4. **Explicit versions**
5. **Observable execution**
6. **Tools require explicit permission**
7. **Model providers are adapters**
8. **Infrastructure is replaceable**
9. **Open-source capability is real capability**
10. **AI Office is built on normal OSVA primitives**
11. **Human approval is a first-class workflow state**
12. **Cost and quality are product data**
